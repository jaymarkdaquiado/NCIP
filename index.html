National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP)

A simple guide to the agency protecting the rights of ICCs/IPs in the Philippines

What is NCIP?

The **National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP)** is the principal government agency in the Philippines responsible for the formulation and implementation of policies, plans, and programs to **promote and protect the rights and well-being** of Indigenous Cultural Communities/Indigenous Peoples (ICCs/IPs).

It was created by **Republic Act No. 8371**, also known as **The Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act (IPRA) of 1997**.

Mandates: The Four Bundles of Rights

The NCIP's mission is founded on four core rights guaranteed by the IPRA Law:

  • **Right to Ancestral Domains and Lands:** Protecting and defending the ownership and possession rights over ancestral domains/lands.
  • **Right to Self-Governance and Empowerment:** Respecting the inherent right of ICCs/IPs to self-determination and their traditional institutions.
  • **Right to Social Justice and Human Rights:** Ensuring human rights protection and preventing discrimination against ICCs/IPs.
  • **Right to Cultural Integrity:** Preserving and protecting the culture, traditions, and historical heritage of Indigenous Peoples.

Programs and Projects

The NCIP delivers critical services to uplift ICCs/IPs. Key programs include:

Ancestral Domain Recognition

Facilitating the issuance of **Certificates of Ancestral Domain Title (CADT)** and Certificates of Ancestral Land Title (CALT).

FPIC Process

Ensuring **Free and Prior Informed Consent (FPIC)** from IPs before any major development project is undertaken in their ancestral domains.

Educational Assistance

Providing scholarships and educational support to qualified IP students to promote access to quality education and opportunities.
